HOW IT WORKS:
-------------------------------------------------------
1. 🔕 100% TRUE SILENT RIDE:
   - Your phone stays completely silent while riding.
   - NO speakerphone, NO noise in your earbuds, NO wind interference.

2. 📵 ENTER & SAVE CUSTOM DUMMY / SWITCHED-OFF NUMBERS:
   - Built-in "Save Number" Feature:
     • Type any 10-digit number (such as an old expired SIM from your drawer, a family member's switched-off phone, or an unallocated dummy number).
     • Enter an optional custom label (e.g. "My Old Jio SIM", "Dad's Spare Keypad").
     • Tap "SAVE" – saved instantly into the app's permanent storage and native Android system!
     • The app maintains a "📁 Saved Numbers Library": tap any saved card to activate it anytime.
     • Tap "📋 COPY" to copy the active number to your clipboard with one touch.
   - Understanding Carrier Announcements:
     • Real turned-off SIM: The carrier IVR announces "The number you are calling is switched off / currently unreachable" (Hindi & English).
     • Unallocated dummy number (e.g. 9800000000): The carrier IVR announces "The number you have dialed is not in service / does not exist".
   - Carrier Format Selector:
     • [GSM / Airtel / Vi / BSNL] (*67*<NUMBER>#)
     • [Jio VoLTE] (*404*<NUMBER>)
     • [Custom Code] (set your own prefix)
   - 100% GUARANTEED SHORTCUT (PHONE SETTINGS):
     • Tap "⚙️ Open Phone Call Settings" directly inside Turbo Bike.
     • In Android Phone Settings -> Call Forwarding -> "When Busy", paste your saved number.
     • This bypasses keypad MMI codes entirely and works 100% on all 4G VoLTE & 5G networks!

3. 📩 INSTANT DUAL-LANGUAGE SMS DELIVERED TO CALLER:
   - Within 1.5 seconds of the call ending, the caller receives this SMS:

   "🏍️ Turbo Bike Mode:
    I am riding a bike right now and cannot answer. If this is an URGENT emergency, please call me 3 times to ring my phone.

    नमस्ते, मैं अभी बाइक चला रहा हूँ। अगर कोई ज़रूरी बात है, तो कृपया मुझे 3 बार कॉल करें।"

4. 🚨 3-CALL EMERGENCY BREAKTHROUGH:
   - If the caller calls 3 times within 5 minutes, Turbo Bike recognizes it as an emergency!
   - The call will NOT be cut and will NOT be forwarded.
   - Your phone overrides silent mode and rings aloud at MAX volume with the emergency siren and saved contact name (Mom, Dad, etc.) so you can pull over and answer safely.

5. 📋 RIDE CALL SUMMARY:
   - When you finish riding and toggle Turbo Bike Mode OFF, the Ride Call Summary appears:
     • Contact Name (e.g. Mom, Dad, Office)
     • Phone Number
     • Call Time (e.g. 10:14 PM)
     • Number of Times Called (e.g. Called 2 times)
     • Green "Call" button next to each person to return their call with 1 tap.

HOW TO ALLOW SMS ON ANDROID 13 & 14 (1-TIME SETUP):
-------------------------------------------------------
Because this app is installed directly (sideloaded):
1. Open phone Settings -> Apps -> Turbo Bike Mode.
2. Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top-right corner.
3. Tap "Allow restricted settings" and confirm with your PIN / fingerprint.
4. Tap Permissions -> SMS -> Allow.
5. Tap Permissions -> Phone & Contacts -> Allow.
